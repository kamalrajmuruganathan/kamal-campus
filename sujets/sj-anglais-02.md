# Bac — Compréhension et expression écrites (anglais)

**Durée conseillée : 1 h 30 — /20.**

## Document

> At sixteen, Maya Osei was not a scientist. She was simply a teenager who loved
> surfing on the coast of Ghana. But every morning, the waves brought more and
> more plastic to the beach: bottles, bags, broken toys. One day, she counted
> more than two hundred pieces of rubbish in a single hour.
>
> "I felt angry," she remembers. "But anger is useless if you do nothing with it."
>
> So Maya started to read. She learned that most ocean plastic comes from rivers,
> not from the sea itself. If you can stop the plastic in the river, you can
> protect the ocean. With three friends and a lot of recycled materials, she
> built a simple floating barrier that catches rubbish before it reaches the
> coast.
>
> The first version broke after two days. The second one sank. The third one
> worked. Today, her small invention collects around fifty kilos of plastic
> every week, and local fishermen help her empty it.
>
> Maya knows she cannot save the whole planet alone. "I am one person with one
> river," she says. "But there are thousands of rivers, and millions of young
> people. Imagine what we could do together."
>
> She now teaches other students how to build their own barriers.

## Comprehension (10 points)

1. Who is Maya Osei and where does she live? (1 pt)
2. What problem does she notice on the beach? Give one precise detail. (2 pts)
3. What did Maya learn about the origin of ocean plastic? (2 pts)
4. Explain the sentence: "The first version broke... The third one worked."
   What does it tell us about her project? (2 pts)
5. What is Maya's message to other young people? (3 pts)

## Written expression (10 points)

Choisis UN sujet et écris 120–150 mots :

- A. Write a short speech to convince students in your school to start an
  environmental project.
- B. "Young people can really change the world." Do you agree? Justify your answer
  with examples.

## Corrigé indicatif

- **Compréhension** :
  1. Une adolescente ghanéenne de 16 ans, passionnée de surf, vivant sur la côte du Ghana.
  2. La quantité de plastique apporté par les vagues : plus de 200 déchets en une heure.
  3. Que la majorité du plastique océanique provient des rivières, pas de la mer.
  4. Que le projet a échoué plusieurs fois avant de réussir : la persévérance / le
     processus d'essai-erreur.
  5. Seule elle ne peut pas tout sauver, mais l'action collective de millions de jeunes
     peut avoir un impact énorme.
- **Expression** :
  - Attendus : impératif et modaux (*we should, we can, let's*) pour le discours ;
    arguments structurés pour l'essai.
  - Connecteurs : *first, moreover, that is why, in conclusion*.
  - Valoriser un exemple concret (Greta Thunberg, projet local, tri des déchets).
