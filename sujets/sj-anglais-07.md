# Bac — Compréhension et expression écrites (anglais)

**Durée conseillée : 1 h 30 — /20.**

## Document

> In a small mining town in the American mountains, the roads often disappeared
> under the snow. Families lived far apart, and many had never held a single
> book. So, in the 1930s, a group of women decided to bring the books to the
> people themselves. They were called the Pack Horse Librarians.
>
> Every week, whatever the weather, they rode horses and mules through frozen
> valleys, carrying saddlebags full of novels, magazines and school textbooks.
> They crossed rivers, climbed narrow paths, and knocked on doors that had never
> seen a stranger for months.
>
> Martha was one of them. She earned very little money, and the work was
> dangerous. Once, her horse slipped on the ice and she nearly fell into a river.
> Yet she never stopped. "A book," she said, "is a window in a house that has no
> windows."
>
> For many children, Martha was the only teacher they would ever meet. For older
> people, who could not read, she would sit by the fire and read the stories
> aloud. Slowly, quietly, these women changed the future of an entire region —
> not with speeches or laws, but with paper, patience and courage.
>
> Today their names are almost forgotten. But every reader in those mountains is,
> in a way, one of their students.

## Comprehension (10 points)

1. Explain who the Pack Horse Librarians were and why they existed. (2 pts)
2. Describe the difficulties of their work, using precise examples from the text. (2 pts)
3. Explain Martha's sentence: "A book is a window in a house that has no windows." (3 pts)
4. In what way did these women "change the future of an entire region"? (2 pts)
5. Comment on the last sentence. Why does the author write it? (1 pt)

## Written expression (10 points)

Choisis UN sujet et écris 150–180 mots :

- A. Imagine you interview Martha for a magazine. Write the questions and her answers.
- B. "One ordinary person can make a real difference in society." Discuss this
  statement using examples from the text and from your own knowledge.

## Corrigé indicatif

- **Compréhension** :
  1. Des femmes qui, dans les années 1930, apportaient des livres à cheval aux familles
     isolées des montagnes minières américaines.
  2. Neige, froid, chemins étroits, rivières ; le cheval de Martha a glissé sur la glace,
     salaire faible, travail dangereux.
  3. Métaphore : un livre ouvre l'esprit et le monde à ceux qui vivent isolés et privés
     d'accès au savoir.
  4. En diffusant la lecture et l'éducation, elles ont transformé durablement la région,
     sans lois ni discours.
  5. Pour rendre hommage à ces femmes oubliées et souligner leur héritage discret mais
     immense.
- **Expression** :
  - Attendus : temps du passé maîtrisés, discours rapporté (sujet A), argumentation
    structurée (B).
  - Lexique : *commitment, literacy, to volunteer, legacy, unsung heroes*.
  - Sujet B : exemples pertinents (Rosa Parks, Malala, bénévoles locaux).
