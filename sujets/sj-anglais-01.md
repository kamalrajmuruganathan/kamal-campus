# Bac — Compréhension et expression écrites (anglais)

**Durée conseillée : 1 h 30 — /20.**

## Document

> When Priya moved from Mumbai to a small town in Wales, she thought the hardest
> part would be the weather. In fact, it was the silence. In India, her street
> was always loud with music, scooters and neighbours calling to each other. In
> Wales, the streets were quiet and grey, and people spoke in short, polite
> sentences she did not always understand.
>
> At school, her classmates were curious but distant. They asked her to say
> things in Hindi, then laughed kindly and moved on. For weeks, Priya ate her
> lunch alone, watching the rain slide down the windows and missing the smell of
> her grandmother's kitchen.
>
> Everything changed on the day of the school cooking competition. Priya decided
> to make samosas, following her grandmother's recipe from memory. The whole
> classroom filled with the warm smell of spices. Suddenly, students who had
> never spoken to her wanted to taste her food and learn how to fold the pastry.
>
> "Food is a language too," her teacher said, smiling. "Sometimes it says more
> than words."
>
> That evening, Priya called her grandmother and told her, for the first time,
> that Wales was beginning to feel a little like home.

## Comprehension (10 points)

1. Where does Priya come from, and where does she move to? (1 pt)
2. According to the text, what surprises her most about her new town? (2 pts)
3. How do her classmates behave towards her at first? Quote the text to support
   your answer. (2 pts)
4. What event changes the situation? Explain in your own words. (2 pts)
5. Explain the sentence: "Food is a language too." What does the teacher mean? (3 pts)

## Written expression (10 points)

Choisis UN sujet et écris 120–150 mots :

- A. Priya writes an email to a friend in India describing her first month in Wales.
  Write the email.
- B. "Sharing food or traditions is a good way to make friends in a new place."
  Do you agree? Give your opinion with examples.

## Corrigé indicatif

- **Compréhension** :
  1. Elle vient de Mumbai (Inde) et déménage dans une petite ville du pays de Galles.
  2. Le silence : contrairement à sa rue bruyante en Inde, les rues galloises sont
     calmes et grises.
  3. Curieux mais distants : "curious but distant", ils lui demandent de parler hindi
     puis "laughed kindly and moved on".
  4. La compétition de cuisine : elle prépare des samosas et l'odeur des épices attire
     ses camarades, qui veulent goûter et apprendre.
  5. La nourriture communique des émotions et une culture sans mots ; elle crée du lien
     là où la langue ne suffit pas.
- **Expression** :
  - Attendus : registre adapté (email amical / essai argumenté), présent et prétérit
    corrects, vocabulaire des émotions et de la culture.
  - Connecteurs utiles : *at first, then, however, finally, in my opinion, for example*.
  - Sujet B : au moins deux arguments et un exemple personnel.
