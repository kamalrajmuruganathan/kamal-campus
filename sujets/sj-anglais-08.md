# Bac — Compréhension et expression écrites (anglais)

**Durée conseillée : 1 h 30 — /20.**

## Document

> When Amir cleared out his late grandfather's flat in Manchester, he found an
> old leather suitcase under the bed. Inside, wrapped in a faded scarf, were a
> ship ticket, a black-and-white photograph, and a letter written in a language
> Amir could not read.
>
> His grandfather had never spoken much about the past. Amir knew only that the
> old man had arrived in England as a young man, alone, with almost nothing. The
> family had always treated his silence as normal — the past was the past, and
> nobody asked questions.
>
> Curious, Amir photographed the letter and sent it to a translator. A week
> later, the reply arrived. The letter was from his grandfather's mother, written
> the night before he left their village forever. She had written: "Go, my son.
> Build a life we could not give you. But never let them tell you that you came
> from nowhere."
>
> Amir sat for a long time with the translation in his hands. Suddenly, the quiet
> old man he had known became a person with a story, a mother, a fear, a hope.
>
> "We inherit more than money or houses," Amir later wrote. "We inherit silences.
> And sometimes, if we are lucky, we get the chance to break them."

## Comprehension (10 points)

1. What does Amir find in his grandfather's flat? (2 pts)
2. How had the family always treated the grandfather's past? (2 pts)
3. What does the translated letter reveal? Quote the key sentence. (2 pts)
4. How does Amir's view of his grandfather change after reading the letter? (2 pts)
5. Explain the final sentences: "We inherit silences... break them." (2 pts)

## Written expression (10 points)

Choisis UN sujet et écris 150–180 mots :

- A. Amir writes a letter to his own future children, telling them what he has
  learned about his family. Write the letter.
- B. "Knowing your family's history helps you understand who you are." Do you
  agree? Develop your point of view with examples.

## Corrigé indicatif

- **Compréhension** :
  1. Une valise en cuir contenant un billet de bateau, une photo en noir et blanc et
     une lettre dans une langue qu'il ne comprend pas.
  2. Comme un sujet normal à ne pas aborder : "the past was the past, and nobody asked
     questions".
  3. Que la mère du grand-père l'a encouragé à partir : "never let them tell you that
     you came from nowhere".
  4. Le vieil homme silencieux devient une personne avec une histoire, une mère, une
     peur, un espoir.
  5. On hérite aussi des non-dits familiaux ; parfois on peut les révéler et comprendre
     son passé.
- **Expression** :
  - Attendus : registre intime (A) ou argumentatif (B) ; maîtrise du present perfect
    et du passé.
  - Lexique : *heritage, migration, roots, belonging, to inherit, ancestors*.
  - Connecteurs : *indeed, in fact, on the whole, as far as I am concerned*.
