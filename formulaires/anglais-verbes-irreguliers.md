Les verbes irréguliers les plus courants : **base verbale (BV)** → **prétérit** → **participe passé (PP)**. Le prétérit sert au passé simple ; le participe passé sert au present perfect (*have + PP*) et au passif (*be + PP*). À apprendre par cœur.

## Les incontournables

| Base | Prétérit | Participe passé | Sens |
|---|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | been | être |
| have | had | had | avoir |
| do | did | done | faire |
| go | went | gone | aller |
| get | got | got / gotten | obtenir |
| make | made | made | fabriquer |
| say | said | said | dire |
| see | saw | seen | voir |
| come | came | come | venir |
| take | took | taken | prendre |
| know | knew | known | savoir |
| give | gave | given | donner |
| find | found | found | trouver |
| think | thought | thought | penser |
| tell | told | told | dire (à qqn) |
| become | became | become | devenir |

## À retenir

- Le **prétérit** ne change jamais avec la personne : *I went, she went*.
- Après **have/has**, on met le **participe passé**, pas le prétérit : *I have seen* (et non « I have saw »).
- Au **passif**, on met aussi le participe passé : *It was written by…*
- Les verbes **réguliers** forment prétérit et participe passé en **-ed** (play → played → played).
