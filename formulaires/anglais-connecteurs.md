Les **linking words** structurent un texte ou un oral et font gagner des points à l'écrit comme à l'oral. Choisis-en un dans chaque catégorie selon ce que tu veux exprimer.

## Ajouter une idée
*and*, *also*, *too*, *as well (as)*, *in addition*, *moreover*, *furthermore*, *besides*, *what is more*.
> **Furthermore**, tourism creates jobs.

## Opposer, contraster
*but*, *however*, *yet*, *nevertheless*, *on the other hand*, *whereas*, *while*, *although* / *though* / *even though*, *in spite of* / *despite* (+ nom ou -ing), *unlike*.
> **Although** it was raining, they went out. — **Despite** the rain, they went out.

## Exprimer la cause
*because*, *because of* (+ nom), *since*, *as*, *due to* / *owing to* (+ nom), *thanks to*.
> They stayed home **because of** the storm.

## Exprimer la conséquence
*so*, *therefore*, *thus*, *as a result*, *consequently*, *that is why*, *so … that*, *such … that*.
> The film was boring, **therefore** we left.

## Exprimer le but
*to* + base verbale, *in order to*, *so as to*, *so that* (+ proposition).
> She works hard **in order to** pass her exams. — Speak up **so that** everyone can hear.

## Situer dans le temps
*first*, *then*, *after that*, *next*, *meanwhile*, *afterwards*, *finally*, *at last*, *as soon as*, *while*, *by the time*, *until*, *once*.
> **First**, gather the facts. **Then**, write a plan. **Finally**, check your work.

## Illustrer, préciser
*for example*, *for instance*, *such as*, *in particular*, *namely*, *that is (to say)*, *in other words*.
> Some animals, **such as** dolphins, are very intelligent.

## Comparer
*like*, *as … as*, *similarly*, *likewise*, *in the same way*, *compared to / with*.

## Donner son opinion
*in my opinion*, *I think / believe (that)*, *to me*, *personally*, *it seems to me that*, *from my point of view*, *as far as I am concerned*.

## Nuancer, concéder
*of course*, *admittedly*, *it is true that*, *to some extent*, *on the whole*, *in a way*, *at least*.

## Conclure, résumer
*to sum up*, *in conclusion*, *to conclude*, *all in all*, *on the whole*, *in short*, *overall*, *all things considered*.

## À retenir

- Après *despite* / *in spite of* : un **nom** ou un verbe en **-ing** (pas de proposition). Après *although* / *though* : une **proposition** (sujet + verbe).
- *because* introduit une proposition ; *because of* est suivi d'un **nom**.
- Varie les connecteurs : ne répète pas *and* et *but* — c'est ce qui distingue un texte de bon niveau.
